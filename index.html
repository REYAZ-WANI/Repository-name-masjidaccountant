Masjid Accountant

Masjid Accountant is an accounting application designed to help masjids and organizations manage their accounting records.

What Masjid Accountant does

  • Manage member names and contribution/payment records
  • Manage tenants, property details, rent and payment records
  • Record income and expense transactions
  • Generate accounting reports and receipts
  • Create backups of accounting data

Google Drive Backup

Masjid Accountant can allow a user to sign in with their Google account and use Google Drive to store and manage the application's backup files. The application requests only the Google Drive file access needed for these backup operations.
