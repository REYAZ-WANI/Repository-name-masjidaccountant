Your privacy matters

Masjid Accountant is designed to keep accounting information under the user's control. Google Drive backup is optional and is used only when the user chooses to connect their Google account.

1About Masjid Accountant

Masjid Accountant is an accounting application designed to help masjids and organizations manage members, contributions, tenants, rent, expenses, transactions, reports, receipts, and accounting records.

2Information We Access

The application may use information that the user enters into the application, such as member, tenant, transaction, payment, income, expense, and accounting information.

3Google Account and Google Drive

Masjid Accountant provides an optional Google Drive backup feature. When a user chooses to connect a Google account, the application uses Google Sign-In to authenticate the user's Google account.


The application requests access to Google Drive files created or used by the application for backup purposes. This access is requested using the Google Drive file scope.


Google account information and Google Drive access are used only to provide authentication and the requested backup and restore functionality.

4How We Use Information

Information is used to provide accounting, reporting, receipt, backup, restore, and related application functionality.

5Data Storage

Accounting information is stored locally by the application. If the user enables Google Drive backup, selected backup files may be stored in the user's Google Drive.

6Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent users' personal information. Information is not shared with third parties except when required to provide functionality requested by the user, such as authentication through Google and storage of requested backup files in Google Drive, or when required by law.

7User Control

Users may choose whether to connect their Google account. Users may also disconnect the Google account from the application.

8Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect application data. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.

10Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or Masjid Accountant, please contact the developer through the contact information associated with the application.
